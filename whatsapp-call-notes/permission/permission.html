Microphone Permission Required

To record your voice during WhatsApp calls, this extension needs access to your microphone.

Why is this needed?
The recording will contain both your voice and the other person's voice. Without microphone access, only the remote person's audio will be recorded.

What happens next:

  1. Click "Allow Microphone Access" above
  2. Your browser will ask for microphone permission
  3. Click "Allow" in the browser prompt
  4. Return to WhatsApp and start recording
